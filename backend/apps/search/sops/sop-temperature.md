# Temperature SOP

## When temperature-sensitive cargo exceeds limit

If a reefer shipment reports a temperature outside the booked band for more than 10 minutes:

1. Stop the vehicle in a safe place.
2. Do not open the cargo doors.
3. Call ops and quote the shipment number and last probe reading.
4. Ops decides whether to divert to the nearest cold store or continue.

Never reset the logger. The temperature SOP is the source of truth for insurance claims.

## Probe checks

Drivers with the reefer skill must log probe readings at pickup and every two hours.
