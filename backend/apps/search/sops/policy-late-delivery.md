# Late delivery policy

## Missed promised_at

If a shipment will miss `promised_at`:

1. Mark it delayed in the ops console.
2. Notify the customer with a new ETA.
3. Do not leave the package unattended at the gate.

Whitefield and Electronic City windows are tight after 18:00. Priority customers (BioLabs) must be called within 15 minutes of the delay flag.
